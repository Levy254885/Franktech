Interior design that works as well as it looks.

Homes, offices, shops and hospitality spaces planned and finished by one team.

A good interior starts with how the space is used. We look at the room, the people and the budget, then plan layouts, materials, colours and lighting that fit together, and we carry the design through to the finished space on site.

What we do

Plan
Space planning and layout options that suit your daily routine and furniture.
Style
Materials, colours and finishes selected to work together.
Detail
Lighting, ceiling, joinery and fixtures coordinated in one scheme.
Deliver

What you receive

  • Space plan and layout options
  • Material, colour and finish selection
  • Lighting and ceiling concepts
  • Guidance on budget and sequencing of works

What you gain

  • One team from concept to handover
  • Fewer surprises on site

Tidy

Partitioning

Partitions that divide space without the mess.

Office, retail and residential partitions built to suit your layout.

Need an extra office, a private meeting room or a cleaner flow through a shop? Partitioning lets you reshape a space quickly. We set out the layout, build the frame, close it in and finish it ready for paint, ceiling and fittings.

What we do

Set out

Frame
Sturdy framing built to suit the height and purpose of each partition.
Close in
Boarding, joints and edges finished smooth and ready for paint.
Integrate
Doors, ceilings and finishes matched to the rest of the space.

What you receive

  • Frame, boarding and jointing
  • Door openings and frames
  • Surface prepared for painting or finishing

What you gain

Solid masonry, built straight and true.

Walls, boundaries, repairs and plastering for homes and commercial buildings.

What we do

Build

Repair
Cracked, damaged or weathered walls repaired and made good.
Plaster
Smooth, even plaster and screed ready for finishing.
Finish
Edges, openings and junctions neatly completed.

What you receive

  • Setting out and wall construction
  • Plastering and screeding
  • Repair of existing masonry
  • Surfaces ready for paint or tile

Tiling

Tiling with tight joints and level floors.

Floors, walls, kitchens and bathrooms tiled neatly in any layout.

Tiling shows every mistake, so the preparation and setting out matter. We check the base, plan the layout so cuts fall in the right places, lay to level and grout cleanly for a finish that lasts.

What we do

Prepare
Base checked and levelled before any tile is laid.
Lay out
Pattern and cuts planned so the result looks balanced.
Fix
Tiles bedded evenly with consistent, tight joints.
Grout
Joints grouted and surfaces cleaned to a neat finish.

What you receive

  • Surface preparation and levelling
  • Floor and wall tiling in your chosen layout
  • Cutting around fixtures and edges
  • Grouting, sealing and clean-up

What you gain

  • Level, easy-to-clean surfaces
  • A balanced, professional layout
  • A finish that stands up to daily use
