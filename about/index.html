About us

A Nairobi contractor for interiors, finishes and construction.

Franktech Enterprises brings the trades that shape a building into one dependable team, so you deal with one contractor from first idea to final handover.

Who we are

Franktech Enterprises is based on Outer Ring Road in Nairobi, Kenya. We specialise in interior design, fitting, partitioning, ceiling works, painting, masonry, tiling and general construction works for homes, offices, shops and other commercial spaces.

Because we handle design, fitting and the building trades ourselves, the work is planned as one job rather than a string of separate contractors. The person who measures your site is part of the team that builds and finishes it, which means fewer misunderstandings and a cleaner result.

We keep our approach simple: listen to what you need, explain the options plainly, quote clearly, then do the work carefully and tidy up after ourselves.
